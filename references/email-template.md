# Reminder email

One email per treatment date, sent 7 calendar days before (settings.lead), 09:00 Africa/Cairo. No email on empty days. Confirmed patients only; doses of patients awaiting approval are listed as held on the Email tab.

Contents are limited to patient name, MRN, drug, dose and week. No diagnosis, BSA, labs or other details.

    Subject: HEMATOLOGY – CHEMOTHERAPY REMINDERS – [Treatment date]

    Dear Clinical Pharmacy Team,

    Chemotherapy scheduled for [Treatment date]:

    1. Patient name: [name]
       MRN: [MRN or "Not recorded"]
       • [Drug]: [dose mg] ([mL] mL at [conc] mg/mL, only when set)[ for N days], week [n]

    Best regards,
    Hematology Clinical Pharmacy Reminder System

Gmail is not connected inside the page: copy or download the email, then "Mark as sent". If a Gmail connector is available in chat, draft or send it there and log the send only after Gmail confirms.
