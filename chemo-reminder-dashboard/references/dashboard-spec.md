# Dashboard: what each part does

Simple, large-type layout (17.5 px base; nothing below 12.5 px). Menu groups: **Daily work** (Overview, Approvals only when someone is awaiting approval, MAR, Calendar, Patients, Email), **Pharmacy & protocols** (Pharmacy, Concentrations, Protocols, References), **Records** (Reminder log, Settings). Global search in the top bar.

- **Overview**: four tiles; modern **week timeline** (8 large day cards, today to day 7, each with date, dose count, patients’ full names in status colour (up to three, then “+N more”) and drug-colour bars; markers for Today, Email day and unavailable drugs; previous/next week and Today buttons; patient dropdown filter; selecting a day opens a vertical timeline of dose cards showing dose mg, volume mL, route, BSA, status, reminder, Open and Postpone); one collapsible **Needs attention** list (missed reminders, shortages, dose discrepancies, data conflicts, patients awaiting approval, doses to confirm); today's email card; patient list.
- **MAR**: columns exactly Patient name · BSA · Week of treatment · Drug name · Dose in mg · Dose in mL, grouped by date; filters (from/to, patient, home oral doses); Print; Export CSV (adds date and status). mL only when a concentration is saved in Pharmacy.
- **Calendar**: 7-day agenda (default) and month view; filters by patient, drug class, status.
- **Patients**: cards with status colour; patient page has header facts, **treatment journey** (protocol phases as milestones, % through the current phase), next 7 days (days with doses only), phase schedules (recent and upcoming rows, "Show earlier doses"), dose modifications, postponements, and collapsible More details (measurement history, record sheet, record-review notes).
- **Status colours with labels**: green Confirmed, red Delayed (a postponed dose is pending), yellow Awaiting approval.
- **Dose window**: confirm given, postpone (reason, name, original date kept, status → Delayed), enter prescribed dose (discrepancy check; authorize with name + justification; never auto-changes a dose), modify dose (tracked: original, new, direction, date, reason, person, documentation), not given, hold, cancel, edit entry, verify, reset, References.
- **Edit mode** on each section (schedule, record sheet, measurements, checklist, protocol card, email text). Saving needs a reason and a name and is logged. Overview sections link to their editable source. Audit records stay read-only.
- **Pharmacy**: per-protocol availability checklist (Available / Unavailable / Not verified). A drug marked Unavailable under any protocol is flagged on every upcoming dose of it (banner on every tab, roadmap, calendar, MAR, patient cards and schedules, list of affected doses). Links to the Concentrations tab.
- **Concentrations**: one row per drug with a single concentration field (mg/mL; units/mL for PEG-asparaginase), optional formulation, "Tablets, no mL" for oral drugs, live preview of the next dose in mL and the number of upcoming doses affected; one Save button with name and optional source; every change is logged. Volumes appear on the MAR, timeline, dose window, patient schedules and email; a drug left blank shows "Set concentration" instead of a volume.
- **Protocols / References**: protocol digest with open questions; References open the exact hospital-sheet page and UKALL 2011 protocol page with the drug highlighted (images in `assets/refs.json`).
- **Email**: one email per treatment date, 7 days ahead, confirmed patients only; fields: patient name, MRN, drug, dose (mg, plus mL when a concentration is set), week. Copy, download, mark as sent, edit text.
- **Settings**: theme, reminder rules, default confirmation toggle, BSA formula, data export/import/reset.
- **Assistant** (needs the `sample` capability): proposes edits from plain language; shows the change; applies only after a named person confirms; logged "via assistant".
- **Saving**: edits stay local until Save changes; the page republishes itself (`artifact` capability). Viewers without edit rights get a read-only view. Declare capabilities `{artifact:{}, downloads:true, sample:{}, user:{}}` on first publish.
